# Second-backbone robustness protocol, before new training

Prior Swin results/reviews are known. This is adaptive within-source robustness, not preregistration or independent-image validation. Exactly nine runs: ResNet-18, ImageNet-1K V1, Soft/Uniform/Tie-Uniform, seeds 17/42/89; order seed then these conditions. No outcome-dependent tuning, extra seeds or conditions.

Retain source, pixel-isolated roles, canonical classes, supported-mass weights, batch averaging, grayscale3 224 bilinear mean/std 0.5, stateless flips, sample order, 12 epochs, batch 32, AdamW lr/decay 1e-4 and cosine minimum 1e-6. Train all parameters including normal BatchNorm updates. A pooled 512-vector receives one bias-free 512→32→512 ReLU/sigmoid gate and biased seven-logit classifier. This matches head principle/placement, not Swin dimensions. No architecture/head superiority or convergence claim follows. Shared normalization intentionally differs from pretrained default ImageNet inference transforms.

Losses equal retained Soft/Uniform/Tie-Uniform. Zero supported mass gives zero loss. Within-seed initialization/order/augmentation must match. Select by ten-category squared distance on selection only, earlier epoch winning ties; retain selected and epoch12. Finish/hash nine runs before calibration/test inference. Resume unchanged; record failures/repairs. Smoke tests use train/selection only, do not tune hyperparameters and are not reported full runs.

Secondary robustness endpoints: Soft-minus-Uniform and Soft-minus-Tie-Uniform at 80% own-ranking test coverage, selected and epoch12, all three seed differences, mean and 5000 paired pixel-cluster conditional bootstrap intervals regardless of direction. Intervals are descriptive, not confirmatory significance tests. Swin primary unchanged. Also report fixed-set cross-evaluation for both contrasts and overlaps, reference/population F1, supported-mass retention, unsupported vote mass, all epoch logs/selected epochs and 20/50/100% coverage sensitivities. No unrun ResNet Hard results.

No new image source, learned mass selector, entropy-matched control, participant study or journal submission. Preserve old releases.
