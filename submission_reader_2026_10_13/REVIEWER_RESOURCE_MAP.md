4.13 GB together and are unnecessary for numerical replay. Their original
model-card instructions are historical; the current README's extraction paths
take precedence. CHECKPOINT_AVAILABILITY.csv identifies each member and its
verified SHA-256. Absolute source-computer paths in provenance records identify
where auditing occurred, not paths a reviewer must possess.

New archives have SHA256SUMS.json, and checkpoint archives have
SHA256_MANIFEST.json. Whole-archive hashes and actual local delivery paths are
recorded separately in the author file index; they cannot include a self-hash
inside the archive they describe. No face media are redistributed. The separate
Manuscript-source.zip contains the complete editable two-document LaTeX build;
Figures.zip contains the twenty-one used vector figures and their evidence map.
