# Scientific provenance and analysis boundary

Title: Crowd Supervision and Vocabulary-Mismatched Selective Facial-Expression Prediction

Authors: Gunjan Kumar Mishra, Bijaya Ghimire, Badri Raj Lamichhane, Alan Shah.
Corresponding author: Badri Raj Lamichhane, d6622300231@g.siit.tu.ac.th.

The TechRxiv v1 identifier is 10.36227/techrxiv.175416003.30236370/v1. Its five
historical weighted-F1 values, manual relabeling, exclusions, targeted
augmentation and participant claims are not current evidence: checkpoints,
predictions and curation records were not retained. The archival
`final_experiment/HISTORICAL_PROVENANCE.md` describes the initial six-run phase,
not the final 24-run study. Historical values remain separately labeled and are
never pooled with the new findings. No public history is overwritten.

Current training has four adaptive phases on the same FER2013/FER+ image source:
six Hard/Soft Swin runs, six Uniform/Tie-hard Swin controls, three Tie-Uniform
Swin controls, and nine Soft/Uniform/Tie-Uniform ResNet runs. Every phase uses
seeds 17, 42 and 89; prior phase outcomes were known. Selection/calibration/test
roles isolate exact pixel hashes, not people or near duplicates. Each run has
selected and epoch-12 states, totaling 48 states, not 48 independent trainings.

The exploratory score sensitivity reuses exposed predictions. The external
RAVDESS protocol was frozen 11 October 2026 before external model outcomes but
after internal outcomes and annotation summaries. Its original freeze and
pre-analysis mapping amendment are preserved; the earlier adapter transposed
disgust/surprise. The primary workbook descriptor/accuracy anchors establish
response IDs 7/8 respectively. This correction predates external scientific
outcomes and does not alter media, accepted IDs or model probabilities.

The 12 October finalization addendum is explicitly post hoc: all external
outcomes were already known. It adds descriptive category frequencies,
vote-weighted matrices, temporal summaries and independent checks of retained
outputs. It adds no training, inference, selector, filtering or inferential test.
All 36 external Soft/Uniform/Tie-Uniform states are retained. Human judgments
are clip-level; frame-to-clip agreement is not frame accuracy.

Scientific Online Resources contain code, manifests, source/role hashes, logs,
predictions, protocols/amendment and current diagnostics. Revision progress,
checklists, cover drafts and author sign-off requests are kept outside them.
No facial images/videos or credentials are redistributed. Internal numerical
verification is not independent external replication.
