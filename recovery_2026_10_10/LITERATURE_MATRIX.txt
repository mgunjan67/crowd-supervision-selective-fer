Closest-work and source-access matrix (checked 10 October 2026)
This is a focused comparison, not a systematic review or exhaustive novelty proof.

Barsoum et al., 2016, Training Deep Networks for Facial Expression Recognition
with Crowd-Sourced Label Distribution.
Primary: https://arxiv.org/html/1608.01041
Official implementation: https://github.com/microsoft/FERPlus/blob/master/src/ferplus.py
Inspected: training/evaluation methods and official vote-processing code.
Established: same-image FER2013 reannotation; majority, multi-label, probabilistic
draw and cross-entropy targets. Original processing differs from our raw ten-vote,
seven-output weighted experiment. New result cannot be advertised as the first
FER crowd-target comparison or directly ranked against its categorical accuracy.

Washington et al., 2021, Training Affective Computer Vision Models by Crowdsourcing
Soft-Target Labels. Cognitive Computation 13:1363-1373.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s12559-021-09936-4
Primary manuscript: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9165031/
Publisher: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12559-021-09936-4
Inspected: published metadata/abstract and accessible author-manuscript methods.
Established: hard/soft ResNet-152 using CAFE's 100-response distributions and
categorical-versus-distribution agreement distinction. Their split is child-
disjoint. Our matched softening/tie controls and own-ranking/fixed-set comparison
are different, but the underlying learning principle/trade-off is not new.
Published author list differs from early arXiv record; use publisher metadata.

Khurana et al., 2024, Crowd-Calibrator, Conference on Language Modeling.
Primary: https://arxiv.org/html/2408.14141v1
Published conference record: https://openreview.net/forum?id=VWWzO3ewMS
Inspected: Sections 2-4, estimator inputs, training criteria, scores and metrics.
Established: learned crowd estimator with distribution-distance rejection and
optional entropy in subjective NLP. Not a FER score baseline that can be copied
from the paper's accuracy table. Our three probability-only scores do not
reproduce its learned estimator; that comparison remains unimplemented.

Le et al., WACV 2023, Uncertainty-aware Label Distribution Learning for FER.
Primary: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2023/html/Le_Uncertainty-Aware_Label_Distribution_Learning_for_Facial_Expression_Recognition_WACV_2023_paper.html
Code: https://github.com/minhnhatvt/label-distribution-learning-fer-tf
Verified: publication/author metadata, primary abstract and implementation README.
Full-method access attempt was blocked (403); do not mark a new reproduction or
complete implementation audit as done. Uses valence-arousal neighborhood target
construction, unlike direct use of observed crowd counts.

Wang, Li and Wang, 2026, arXiv:2609.17130 (unpublished preprint).
Primary: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17130v1
Inspected: introduction and Methods 3.1-3.5; full results/code audit incomplete.
Vote-count Dirichlet-multinomial modeling and rejection in dynamic FER are close
in motivation. Do not claim priority over it, or treat its reported percentage
improvements as verified comparative evidence for our static-image experiment.

Fard et al., AffectNet+, earlier accessible manuscript v1 (2024).
Primary: https://arxiv.org/html/2410.22506v1
Current official access: https://mohammadmahoor.com/pages/databases/affectnetplus/
Inspected: label-generation methods and official access page. Ensemble/AU-based
generated soft labels are not per-image independent human vote fractions. Page
requires a PI/lab manager/professor signed request. This route was not pursued
after user ruled out restricted datasets. Not interchangeable with FER+ votes.

Shin et al. 2024, face-specific label distribution learning,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.imavis.2024.104901
Xu, Gan and Xia 2024, uncertainty-adaptive FER,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patrec.2024.04.015
Publisher abstract/preview only inspected. Neither is represented as a fully
verified method reproduction. Their reported accuracy is not comparable to our
complete-vote disagreement or reference-specific F1. No unnecessary citation
was added solely to inflate the bibliography.
