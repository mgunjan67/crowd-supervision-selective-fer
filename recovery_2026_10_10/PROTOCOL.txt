Retained-prediction selector sensitivity: analysis plan, 10 October 2026

Status at writing: prior MSP outcomes are known; alternative-score results have
not yet been computed for this revision. This is an exploratory, post hoc
analysis of an already exposed test population, not prospective confirmation.
No additional training, model selection or deployment threshold fitting occurs.

Question: does the direction of the Soft-minus-Uniform/Tie-Uniform own-ranking
comparison depend on the confidence functional applied to frozen probabilities?

Include all 24 existing fits, both selected and epoch-12 states (48 states).
Primary population is the unchanged exact-pixel-isolated 3,275-row test role.
The probability-only scores, all larger-is-more-confident, are:
1. MSP = max_c p_c.
2. Margin = largest p minus second-largest p.
3. Negative entropy = sum_c p_c log(p_c), with 0 log 0 = 0.
Use retained float probabilities without renormalizing or calibrating them.
Sort decreasing score, with increasing original row ID to resolve exact ties.
Report ceil(coverage*N) retained images at coverage 0.2, 0.5, 0.8, 1.0.
Endpoint: mean(1 - q[predicted category]), using all ten annotation categories.
Also retain its unsupported-mass, supported-ambiguity and excess components.
All compared conditions use identical test rows/votes, but their accepted sets
can differ. These are not fixed-image predictor comparisons.

Report every seed (17, 42, 89), family, condition and state. Main-text table:
80% Soft-minus-Uniform and Soft-minus-Tie-Uniform paired seed means, in pp.
Supplement: all paired seeds at 80%, and a mean-plus-seed-point sensitivity
figure for Soft-minus-Uniform. Machine-readable ledger: all scores/coverages,
including Hard and Tie-hard where those models exist. No new significance
test or interval; seed ranges are descriptive, not confidence intervals.
No selection of a preferred score or favorable subset after viewing outcomes.
No test-derived threshold is proposed for the messaging prototype.

Verification before interpretation: known-answer tests for all three scores,
zero-probability entropy, exact tie handling, input rejection, permutation
invariance by row ID, ceil counts, 100% identity, and risk decomposition.
Independently match every MSP common-coverage row to the original frozen
ledger, then check its SHA256 is unchanged. Record all NPZ/source hashes.

Interpretation branches:
- Same signs across scores: limited score sensitivity within tested scores,
  not proof against learned selectors, training budgets or other datasets.
- Reversed signs: supervision effect is score-dependent in this setting.
- Small mixed seed differences: state them without equivalence claims.
- Input/audit mismatch: stop interpretation; repair provenance first.

Out of scope: new datasets or annotations, dataset agreements, GPU training,
Crowd-Calibrator reproduction, significance hunting, revised acceptance claims,
and public pushes/uploads. External-data and learned-selector experiments need
a separate bounded protocol and author/resource decisions. Earlier four
adaptive training phases remain four; this is an additional diagnostic analysis.
