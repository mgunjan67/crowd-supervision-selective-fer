# Affiliation institution and location metadata audit

Checked 20 October 2026 (Asia/Katmandu) against the institutions' own pages. This is a narrow name/location check of the four already-confirmed affiliations. It does not reopen the authors' authority to use their affiliations, request further author confirmation or change any ethics declaration.

## Outcome

No unambiguous institution-name, city or country mismatch was found in the current `source/manuscript.tex`. **No manuscript affiliation was changed.** In particular, the current official WorldQuant University contact and privacy pages support **Washington, D.C., United States**, not a replacement with New Orleans.

## WorldQuant historical-address distinction

The [older terms-of-use page](https://www.wqu.edu/terms-of-use) still includes a New Orleans, Louisiana address for a copyright agent. That does not override the current contact page and current privacy policy, which independently state Washington, D.C. as the university's address. A move to New Orleans in this manuscript would therefore be unsupported by the current institutional contact metadata.
